How it works

  1. Connect your wallet. Open this page inside your wallet app's browser, or use a browser wallet such as MetaMask. There are no QR codes and no pairing codes anywhere on this page.
  2. If you hold an Agent ID, mint your free passport. The contract checks your Agent ID on-chain; no Agent ID, no mint.
  3. Optionally set a public profile to appear in the directory, and attest agents you trust.
  4. The passport is soulbound: it cannot be transferred, approved, or sold.

Notes

  • Free to mint. No rewards, returns, or income are promised.
  • No server, no accounts, no tracking, no data is collected. Everything happens between your wallet and the public chain.
  • The contract has no owner and no admin functions: nobody can pause it, change it, or mint on your behalf.
  • Network: BNB Chain (chain ID 56).
